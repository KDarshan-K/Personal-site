## Research Overview

Deep learning sequence recognition models widely utilized in visual security verifications (e.g., CAPTCHA systems and optical character recognition) exhibit critical vulnerabilities when subjected to imperceptible input perturbations. 

This research investigates:
- **Perturbation Susceptibility:** Evaluating boundary vulnerability across single-step Fast Gradient Sign Method (FGSM) and iterative Projected Gradient Descent (PGD) attacks.
- **Vulnerability Propagation:** Analyzing how perturbation noise in early convolutional feature extractors propagates through dense sequence classification heads.
- **Defensive Retraining:** Demonstrating how incorporating bounded gradient perturbations into the training objective restores model classification robustness without degrading baseline accuracy on clean inputs.
