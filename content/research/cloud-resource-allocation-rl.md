## Research Overview

Dynamic resource management in multi-tenant cloud data centers requires balancing competing objectives: minimizing aggregate server power consumption while preventing Service Level Agreement (SLA) violations and respecting tenant isolation policies.

This paper explores:
- **MDP Formulation:** Structuring heterogeneous host servers and dynamic VM workloads into a Markov Decision Process (MDP).
- **Multi-Objective Reward Engineering:** Synthesizing an adaptive reward function that penalizes active idle power draw, migration latency overheads, and co-location security policy breaches.
- **Agent Policy Performance:** Comparing deep reinforcement learning policy agents against standard industry heuristics (Best-Fit Decreasing, First-Fit), demonstrating improved energy efficiency and reduced SLA drop-off under variable load spikes.
