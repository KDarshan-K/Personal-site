## Problem

Static cloud resource allocation can waste compute capacity and increase cost and energy use. This project explores whether reinforcement learning can make allocation decisions more adaptive.

## Exploration

The design considers utilization, cost, SLA compliance, security, and energy as competing objectives. Candidate algorithms include DQN, PPO, and DDPG.

## Next steps

Document the simulator, dataset, reward function, baselines, and measured results after experiments are reproducible.
