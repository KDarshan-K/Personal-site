## Overview

FinRAG explores how retrieval-augmented generation can help users ask questions about financial documents and information.

## Areas of focus

- Document ingestion, chunking, and retrieval.
- Vector search and context grounding.
- Financial calculations and structured data access.
- Evaluation of answer quality and source support.
