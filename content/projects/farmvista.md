## Overview

FarmVista was developed by **Team TwoOne** during **IEEE HackaPhasia 2025**, securing **4th runner-up among 125 competing engineering teams**. The project targets agricultural supply-demand mismatches by combining open government agricultural datasets with predictive machine learning models to assist farmers in making data-driven crop selection and soil treatment decisions.

## Key Capabilities

- **Market Demand Forecasting:** Analyzes historical mandi (produce market) price trends and seasonality to project local crop demand and identify profitable harvest windows.
- **NPK Soil Nutrient Optimization:** Uses a trained **Random Forest Classifier** to assess soil Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K) levels alongside regional rainfall and temperature data to recommend optimal crops and fertilizer balancing.
- **Mobile-First Farmer Experience:** Designed an intuitive, multilingual-ready mobile interface in **Flutter**, prioritizing rapid data entry and clear visual summaries of crop viability.
- **Data Integration Pipeline:** Preprocessed and normalized public datasets from the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare to ensure high inference reliability across diverse geographic zones.

## Tech Stack

- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-Learn (Random Forest, Decision Trees), Pandas, NumPy
- **Mobile Frontend:** Flutter (Dart)
- **Backend / Data Pipeline:** Python, REST APIs
- **Competition:** IEEE HackaPhasia 2025 (4th Runner-Up / 125 Teams)
